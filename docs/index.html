Quick Start

Add a script tag, drop a div, refresh. Widget renders in Shadow DOM.

<!-- Load any beverage widget script -->
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/cocktailfyi-embed@1/dist/embed.min.js"></script>
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/beerfyi-embed@1/dist/embed.min.js"></script>

<!-- Drop widgets -->
<div data-cocktailfyi="entity" data-slug="margarita"></div>
<div data-beerfyi="entity" data-slug="american-ipa"></div>

FAQ

How many cocktails does CocktailFYI cover?

CocktailFYI covers 636 cocktails with ingredients, glassware, garnish, technique, ABV/calorie calculation, IBA classification, 15 family categories. Recipe scaling supported.

What beer styles does BeerFYI document?

BeerFYI documents 112 BJCP 2021 styles. 82 hop varieties, 41 malts, yeast strains. IBU/SRM/OG/FG ranges per style. Food pairings.

Which wine regions does VinoFYI cover?

VinoFYI covers wine regions worldwide with appellation hierarchies (DOC/DOCG/AOC/AVA/GI). Grape varieties, wineries, vintage chart, terroir, food pairing matrix.

What sake grades does NihonshuFYI explain?

NihonshuFYI explains all 6 special designations (特定名称酒): Junmai, Honjozo, Ginjo, Daiginjo, Junmai Ginjo, Junmai Daiginjo. 80 sake, 50 breweries, rice polishing ratios (seimaibuai), parallel multiple fermentation.

How does BrewFYI categorize coffee?

BrewFYI categorizes 72 coffee varieties (Arabica/Robusta cultivars) and 21 brewing methods (espresso, pour over, AeroPress, V60, Chemex, French press, siphon). SCA cupping protocol with flavor wheel.

Why a family of 7 sites?

Each beverage has dedicated domain depth: CocktailFYI for mixology, VinoFYI for oenology, BeerFYI for BJCP brewing, WhiskeyFYI for distillation, NihonshuFYI for Japanese rice wine, TeaFYI for tea science, BrewFYI for coffee. Cross-references for cocktail ingredients.
